Run your own DMR network from one binary

DMRHub is a free, open-source DMR master server. Hotspots, repeaters and other networks connect to it, and it routes private and group calls, texts and positions between them. Use it for a club, a net or an emergency deployment.

A DMR radio in your browser

The DMRHub Browser Radio turns any phone or computer into a DMR radio on your DMRHub network. Sign in with your DMRHub account and it connects as its own hotspot. No radio, modem or codeplug needed.

  • Key up on any talkgroup or private call, and see who is talking now
  • Send and read text messages to radios on the network
  • Share your position and see stations on a map
  • Send and receive raw DMR data from a console

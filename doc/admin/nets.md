# Nets

The **Nets** page in the **Admin** menu (`<siteurl>/admin/nets`) lists every net and every scheduled net. For how users and NCOs run a net, see [Nets](../guides/nets.md).

The **Nets** table shows the ID, the talkgroup, the status (**Active** or **Ended**), the start time and the number of check-ins of each net. The **Scheduled Nets** table shows the name, the talkgroup, the schedule and whether the schedule is on.

- Turn on the **Showcase** switch of a net or a scheduled net to show it at the top of the public **Nets** page.
- Click **Edit** in the row of a scheduled net to change it, or **Delete** to delete it.
